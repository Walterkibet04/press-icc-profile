`/app/chart.csv` holds measurements of one offset press. Each row is a printed patch: the ink zone `zone` it sits in (1 to 8 across the sheet), the ink amounts `C`, `M`, `Y`, `K` in percent and the measured colour `L`, `a`, `b` (CIE Lab, D50, relative to the unprinted paper, so blank paper reads 100, 0, 0). The ink keys were not set evenly when the chart was printed, so in each zone every ink went down with a slightly different film thickness. The profile is for the press with each ink at its average film over the eight zones, and that sheet-average press is the one the grader's hidden model describes. The readings carry about 0.25 CIEDE2000 of instrument noise, and some patches were misread: a few are far off and others are wrong by only a few units, so not every bad reading stands out.

Write an ICC profile for this press to `/app/press.icc`. The grader reads it with its own strict parser, so the file must follow this layout exactly. It is ICC version 4.x with profile class `prtr`, colour space `CMYK`, PCS `Lab `, signature `acsp`, D50 illuminant, a header size field equal to the file size, and a file under 8 MB. The required tags are `desc` and `cprt` (type `mluc`), `wtpt` (type `XYZ `), `A2B1` (type `mAB `) and `B2A1` (type `mBA `); other tags are ignored, and tag offsets must be 4-byte aligned and inside the file.

`A2B1` maps C, M, Y, K to Lab, where 0 to 1 means 0 to 100% ink. It has 4 inputs and 3 outputs and uses the lutAToBType structure with A curves, a CLUT and B curves; the matrix and M curve offsets must be 0. The CLUT is 16-bit with 2 to 17 grid points per input, first input varying slowest. `B2A1` maps Lab to C, M, Y, K. It has 3 inputs and 4 outputs and uses the lutBToAType structure with B curves, a CLUT with 2 to 33 grid points per input (order L, a, b, first slowest) and A curves, again with no matrix or M curves. Every curve must be of type `curv` with 0 entries (identity), 1 entry (gamma) or a table of at most 4096 entries. Lab in the PCS uses the ICC v4 16-bit encoding, L = v/65535 × 100 and a, b = v/65535 × 255 − 128, both at the CLUT output of `A2B1` and at the Lab input of `B2A1`. CLUTs are interpolated n-linearly and inputs are clamped.

The grader has a hidden model of the same press, and all errors are CIEDE2000 with kL = kC = kH = 1. Only `A2B1` is compared with the press. Every `B2A1` check takes the CMYK that `B2A1` returns and runs it back through your own `A2B1`, read by the same parser, so `B2A1` is judged as an inverse of your `A2B1` and an error in your press model is counted once, in the forward check. All of the following must hold. Forward: `A2B1` evaluated at 2,000 hidden ink combinations drawn uniformly from all CMYK with total ink (C+M+Y+K) at most 300% must match the colours of the hidden sheet-average press with mean error at most 0.60 and 95th percentile at most 1.80. Inverse: for 1,264 hidden colours that the press can print within 300% total ink, spread by lightness (about 170 in each 10-unit band of L from 0 to 70, 74 from 70 to 80 and 10 from 80 to 90), `A2B1(B2A1(target))` must be within a mean error of 0.65 and a 95th percentile of 1.80 of the target. Ink limit: `B2A1` must return at most 300.5% total ink for 3,500 hidden Lab values, which include very dark colours and colours far outside the gamut, and for every colour used in the other `B2A1` checks. Gamut clipping: 500 hidden colours lie between 1.5 and 4.5 outside what the press can print within 300% total ink. For each, the grader searches for the colour closest to the target that your `A2B1` produces from any CMYK with total ink at most 300% (a dense ink sample, then local refinement), and `A2B1(B2A1(target))` may be farther from the target than that colour by at most 0.55 on average and 0.90 at the 95th percentile; landing closer than the grader's search counts as 0. Neutrals: for 14 targets with a = b = 0 and L between 14 and 92, `A2B1(B2A1(target))` must be within error 1.0 of the target and have |a| and |b| at most 0.8. Smoothness: for 600 pairs of printable colours that are 1.5 apart, spread by lightness the same way (about 80 pairs in each 10-unit band of L from 0 to 70, 24 from 70 to 80 and 6 from 80 to 90), the `B2A1` results may differ by at most 0.60 in any ink channel, and by more than 0.16 in at most 5% of the pairs.
